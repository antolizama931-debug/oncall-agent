    Policy decision
    Engineering judgment

    The decisions behind
    the demo.

    The portfolio explains trade-offs, not only features—so reviewers can assess the engineering process.

    A

    Why structured evidence?

    Free-form context is difficult to test. Typed evidence records make source, timestamp, relevance, and uncertainty explicit.

    B

    Why approval gates?

    Confidence is not authorization. Operational risk determines whether an action is automatic, reviewed, or blocked.

    C

    Why deterministic replay?

    Frozen inputs and execution traces make regressions reproducible across prompts, models, and policy versions.

    D

    What is intentionally omitted?

    The public sandbox has no production credentials, shell access, or destructive tools. Those boundaries are part of the design.
